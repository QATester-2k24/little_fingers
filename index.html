📲 Play without internet

  1. Open this page in Safari. If you see an ✕ at the top-left, you are inside another app: tap ⋯ then Open in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button (a square with an arrow ⬆️) at the top of Safari.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
  4. Open Little Learners from the Home Screen once with internet. You will see a green "Saved!" message.
After that it works with Wi-Fi off, in airplane mode, anywhere. 🎉

👨‍👩‍👧 Grown-ups only

🔒 Child lock

  1. Open a game and hold the 🔒 button for 1.5 seconds. The child is now stuck in that game: no Home button, Back and refresh are blocked, the screen stays on and it goes full screen.
  2. To unlock, hold 🔓 for 2 seconds and answer the grown-up sum.
Important: a website cannot block the device's own buttons (Home button, app switcher, swipes, closing tabs). For a full lock, use the built-in one:
• iPad / iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Guided Access → On (set a passcode). Open the app, then triple-click the Home / top button → Start.
• Android: Settings → Security → App pinning → On. Open the app, Recents → tap its icon → Pin.
• Chrome on a laptop: press F11 for full screen; for a true kiosk start Chrome with --kiosk.
Tip: Add to Home Screen first, so there is no browser bar to tap.
